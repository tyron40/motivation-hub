## 📱 How to Access Your App on TestFlight

### Step 1: Wait for Apple's Email (5-10 minutes)

You will receive an email from Apple with subject:
**"Your build is ready for testing"**

This email will be sent to the email associated with your Apple Developer account.

### Step 2: Install TestFlight App

If you don't have it already:

1. Open **App Store** on your iPhone/iPad
2. Search for **"TestFlight"**
3. Download and install (it's free from Apple)

## 🔍 Troubleshooting

### "I didn't receive the email"

1. Check spam/junk folder
2. Wait up to 30 minutes
3. Check App Store Connect directly
4. Verify email in Apple Developer account settings

### "TestFlight says 'No apps available'"

1. Make sure you're signed in with correct Apple ID
2. Check if you're added as a tester in App Store Connect
3. Try the public link method instead

### "App won't install"

1. Make sure you have iOS 13.4 or later
2. Check you have enough storage space
3. Try restarting your device
4. Delete old version if you have one

### "Ads aren't showing"

This is normal! Ads will appear after:
1. You add website to App Store Connect
2. AdMob verification completes (15-60 min)
3. You reinstall or relaunch the app
